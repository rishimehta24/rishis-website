About

I build software for senior care. Ascenix takes the clinical notes care teams already write and turns them into incident reports, compliance checks and a clear picture of resident risk. It's live in 55 facilities across Canada and the US, and we're a team of seven.

I got here by moving into a retirement home straight out of high school. Ayaan Esmail, my cofounder, and I lived there for about a year and a half: slept there, ate with the residents, worked from the building. That's where most of what I know about this industry comes from.

I'm from Toronto. I'm in San Francisco now for Y Combinator, and most of our new demand is coming from the US, so that's where the focus is.

Why senior care

I believe in a better tomorrow for the elderly, and I believe the status quo isn't good enough.

Everyone gets old. Most of us will end up in a care home or have a parent in one, and the way those places run today is held together by overworked staff, paper, and luck. I've seen it up close. I lived in one.

Living there is what made this personal. You eat with people every day, you learn their names and their stories, and then you watch a fall go unnoticed or a report eat an afternoon that should have gone to them. You stop seeing it as a market and start seeing it as a debt.

So I keep trying ideas. A watch, a sensor, a wearable, a patent. Most of them failed. Ascenix is the one that worked, and it's still just the first piece. I don't plan on doing anything else.

Ascenix

The incident intelligence layer for long-term care.

Care teams already document everything: falls, behaviours, hydration, assessments, care plans. It's fragmented across thousands of notes, and government mandates say it all has to be analysed anyway. Ascenix plugs into the EHR homes already use, reads that documentation, and sends back the reporting, the compliance gaps and the ranked risk. Nothing gets written twice.

We're hiring engineers and account executives. If you want to work on something that matters to real people, email me directly. More at ascenix.co.

How I operate

Live up to it.

The thing I actually worry about isn't failing. It's not using what I was given.

Go live there.

You don't understand a problem from a deck or a discovery call. I understand senior care because I ate breakfast with residents for a year and a half.

Money is a tool.

I've never cared much about things. Money is useful because it makes things happen faster.

No retirement.

Building a company is the most fulfilled version of me I've found. I don't picture stopping.

Say it plainly.

Generic, over-polished or needlessly complicated answers get pushed back on. If it fits in one sentence, it gets one.

Discipline over mood.

Less TV, fewer distractions, protect the focus. Motivation is nice; it isn't the plan.
