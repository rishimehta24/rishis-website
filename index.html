Why senior care

I believe in a better tomorrow for the elderly, and I believe the status quo isn't good enough.

I've been building in senior care for half a decade and pivoted a few times along the way, so it's a fair question: why this industry?

Because it's one of the most underserved markets in the world relative to how much it matters. The number of elderly people will double by 2050, and we don't have the infrastructure to handle that. The quality of life in most care settings isn't good enough today, and it's about to be stretched much further.

The reason I love being a founder is that a company lets you build something that makes a real difference in a large number of people's lives. Senior care is where that lever is biggest. Changing the way care is given changes daily life for a huge portion of the population, and for everyone who loves them.

I've also always believed it's very difficult to build something great without fully immersing yourself in it. That's why I moved into a retirement home. Everyone gets old, and most of us will end up in a care home or have a parent in one. I've seen how those places run up close, and I don't plan on working on anything else.
